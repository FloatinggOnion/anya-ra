# Anya v0.2.0 — Spatial Thinking

Anya now brings research, questions, and original work together in a canvas-first desktop workspace.

## What's new

- **Connected thinking space:** arrange questions, claims, applications, experiments, papers, notes, documents, conversations, and saved searches on one canvas.
- **Focused tools:** open objects for reading, PDF annotation, writing, and conversation alongside the canvas. Connect ideas with related, supports, contradicts, and cites relationships.
- **Socratic companion:** use selected objects and their immediate connections as context for proposed research steps. Review and accept proposals onto the canvas, or undo an accepted addition.
- **A calmer interface:** scholarly light and dark themes, a new Anya logo, consistent SVG icons, and reorganized Appearance, Writing, AI models, and Privacy settings.
- **More reliable persistence:** isolated conversations, saves that stay with their workspace, and separate pending saves for documents and notes.
- **macOS development fix:** icon changes now trigger a rebuild so the Dock reflects the current logo during `pnpm tauri dev`.

Your workspace remains local. Existing paper discovery, PDF highlights and annotations, notes, document editing, exports, and local/cloud model support remain available.
