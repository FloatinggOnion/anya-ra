## Design Context

### Users
People applying research practically or developing original bodies of work. Their core loop is to investigate a question, connect evidence, challenge assumptions, and develop an application or original argument.

### Brand Personality
Calm, scholarly, inquisitive. Anya is a thoughtful Socratic colleague who offers questions and proposed actions for acceptance.

### Aesthetic Direction
A spacious research canvas with warm paper, ink, muted green accents, serif headings and readable sans-serif controls. Support light and dark modes. Connected objects open focused editors; tools remain available without leaving the thinking space.

### Design Principles
- Make relationships between questions, evidence, and applications visible.
- Keep reading and writing focused, with quiet surrounding controls.
- Distinguish evidence from proposals and unanswered questions.
- Require acceptance before applying agent proposals; never silently edit writing.
- Preserve local workspaces and existing research tools.
